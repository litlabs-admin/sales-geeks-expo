# Final App — 20 Things It Will Have

A simple list of the 20 things the SalesGeek Scotland event companion app will do by the time it ships for Scottish Growth Expo 2026.

1. **One event URL** that works in three modes — before the event, on the day, and after the event (archive).

2. **QR-led entry** — attendees scan a QR at the desk and land straight in the app.

3. **Pre-registration** — attendees can sign up before the event and pick up where they left off on the day.

4. **Email OTP login** — passwordless attendee identity using email as the key, with OTP for prize eligibility.

5. **Auto check-in** — first event-day entry checks the attendee in automatically; staff can override.   

6. **Profile QR** — every attendee has their own QR so staff can identify them fast at the desk.

7. **Home screen** — shows what's live now, what's next, announcements, plus the attendee's points, rank, and progress.

8. **Agenda** — full event schedule with filters by stage, category, and type, and live session status.

9. **Geeks tab** — the four host SalesGeeks with photos, bios, and contact/calendar links.

10. **QR scanning game** — sponsor QRs, session QRs, and hidden bonus QRs that auto-award points on scan, with one-scan-per-attendee rules.

11. **Hidden bonus codes** — time-released, zone-hinted bonus QRs that unlock through the day.

12. **Sponsor pages** — personalized per attendee with scan status, "I'm interested" button, and consent-gated lead sharing.

13. **Rewards catalog** — self-service and staff-only rewards with inventory, locked/sold-out/redeemed states, and per-attendee limits.

14. **William premium reward** — limited-slot post-event strategy session, completed via Calendly booking confirmation.

15. **Anonymous leaderboard** — top 10 plus the attendee's own rank, using aliases only, with a visible prize deadline.

16. **In-app notifications** — immediate and scheduled announcements, delivered inside the app (no browser push).

17. **FAQs and help** — searchable, structured help content editable on event day.

18. **Admin panel** — full control of events, agenda, speakers, Geeks, sponsors, QRs, rewards, FAQs, notifications, and branding.

19. **Live ops dashboard + staff tools** — real-time event-day view for admins plus a staff workflow for redemptions, attendee search, and predefined point actions, with audit logging.

20. **Reporting, exports, and post-event archive** — CSV/JSON exports for attendees, sponsor leads, leaderboard, scans, and rewards, plus a clean archive transition with 10-day attendee access.
